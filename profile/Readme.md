## Overview

Star Wars Genesis is a large-scale, free, fan-made total conversion project built on Starfield's engine and framework. Rather than simply replacing the game's visuals, it reshapes its quests, characters, locations, dialogue, equipment, and gameplay systems into a distinct Star Wars-inspired experience.

The project combines original content, commissioned assets, community contributions, custom patches, and gameplay enhancements into one curated modlist.

Genesis also has its own evolving lore and fictional universe, separate from official Star Wars canon.

## A Different Galaxy

The conversion extends throughout the game world, replacing familiar elements with recognizable Star Wars-inspired environments, factions, equipment, and technology.

Key areas of the experience include:

- **Faction Storylines** — Join the Empire or the Rebellion and progress through their dedicated questlines.
- **Original Narrative Content** — Experience rewritten quests, new characters, dialogue, objectives, and additional storylines.
- **Converted Locations** — Explore redesigned environments and recognizable destinations, including Coruscant, Tatooine, Mandalore, Geonosis, Hoth, and Tython.
- **Custom Equipment** — Discover Star Wars-inspired weapons, spacesuits, gear, and other items.
- **Gameplay Systems** — Expanded RPG mechanics, progression, crafting, balancing, and combat improvements.
- **Visual and Audio Overhaul** — Redesigned interfaces, environmental replacements, custom assets, and curated music integration.

## Story and Exploration

Genesis is designed around a combination of structured narrative content and open-ended exploration.

The currently documented experience includes:

- Acts 1–2 of the main storyline.
- Complete Rebel and Imperial faction questlines.
- 22 side quests.
- New and reimagined locations throughout the galaxy.
- Unique equipment, crafting, exploration, and character progression.
- Lightsabers and basic Force abilities.

The project is still in active development. Its storylines, locations, and gameplay systems continue to expand through major updates.

## Curated Modlist Architecture

Genesis is not intended to be assembled manually from individual Nexus Mods downloads.

Its installation is built around **Wabbajack** and a dedicated portable **Mod Organizer 2** environment. This allows the project to deploy its extensive collection of modifications, custom patches, configurations, and load-order settings as one coordinated setup.

The Nexus Mods page serves as a repository for project-specific files and patches. These files are not a standalone installation package and must not be installed individually.

The majority of the experience is built around original Genesis content, complemented by selected assets and contributions from other mod authors.

## Compatibility and Requirements

The official installation documentation specifies the following requirements:

- **Game:** The Steam version of Starfield.
- **Game Version:** Starfield 1.16.244, as specified by the current installation guide.
- **DLC:** Shattered Space.
- **Platform:** Windows PC.
- **Storage:** Internal NVMe SSD for both Starfield and Genesis.
- **Installation:** Official Genesis installer, Wabbajack, and the included Mod Organizer 2 environment.
- **Language:** English is required for the included voice lines.
- **Save:** Start a new game after installing Genesis.

Genesis is not compatible with:

- Xbox or Bethesda Creations installations.
- Microsoft Store and PC Game Pass versions of Starfield.
- Vortex-based installations.
- Existing Creation Store or unrelated Vortex mod configurations.

An internal NVMe SSD is required by the official guide. The author also recommends an 80 GB Windows page file for performance and stability.

## Important Project Notes

- Genesis is a work in progress, not a completed standalone Star Wars game.
- It requires the original Starfield game and cannot run independently.
- The project is designed for single-player gameplay.
- The supported language is English.
- Adding, removing, or updating individual mods can break custom patches and core functionality.
- The official support process is intended for unmodified Genesis installations.
